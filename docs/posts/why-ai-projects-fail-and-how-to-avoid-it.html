\n

Common Signs That Your AI Project Is Heading Toward Failure

\n

Early red flags include vague problem statements, scope creep, and unrealistic timelines. Teams that run out of clearly defined metrics or pivot without data often find the solution drifting from business value.

\n

Root Causes of AI Project Failure

\n

Data quality issues, poorly selected models, and insufficient integration with existing workflows are frequent culprits. Lack of stakeholder alignment and unclear ownership can also sabotage effort, turning promising pilots into abandoned experiments.
